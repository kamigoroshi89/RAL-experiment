## Obiettivo

Implement the measured half of the runtime safety monitor in `ralmon.monitor`: causal past-time evaluation of the template instances of a care plan on the grid of period `T_s`, robustness intervals in which every unknown future value is replaced by the physiological bounds of the registry, three-valued verdicts (`PENDING`, `VIOLATED`, `SATISFIED`; the fourth state is added by WS05-T08), a bounded reorder buffer, a reported per-template latency bound and a reported cost per sample. Concrete output: `StreamingMonitor` (the `Monitor` protocol), the measured-only `run_offline`, the point-robustness entry point `make_point_evaluator` that baselines B3 and B5 call, and the tests that reproduce the golden cases of WS01. Definitive verdicts come from the measured prefix only (ADR-008); this task contains no forecaster and no conformal code.

### Completion note (worker, 2026-10-09)

Status is left at `in_progress` for the reviewer; nothing is merged. Branch `ws05-t04-monitor-core`. All numbers below were measured in this session.

- **Instance model and decision instants** (docs/formal/05 sections 1 to 6, WS01-T02 sections 1 and 4). Dynamic instances are created at their anchoring event of the finalised step: RANGE and SYS at a MEASUREMENT, HOLD, COUNT limit, RESP and INT at a DISPENSE (`k_d = k_a + B`, with `B = 0` except RESP `delta2`); static instances come from `CarePlan.instances` (SCHED at its tick, the day-end COUNT from the start of its day to the closing step, anchor a positive multiple of `T_day`). The interval is the closed form of Lemma 10 per template, not a deque (deviation 3). VIOLATED iff `hi < 0`; SATISFIED iff `lo > 0`, or `lo = hi = 0` at `k >= k_d`; otherwise PENDING. A definitive state is not re-evaluated.
- **Emission rule** (WS01-T05, 05 section 6.5). One record per active instance per finalised step, in creation order; an instance retires at its decision step, an undecided one at `k_d`, SCHED at its horizon `H = max(k_d, min(k_next, day_close))` while its interval is refined (graded lateness). Stricter than the task text "at activation and at each state change" (deviation 9).
- **CP3** (ties): the tie rule above; `lo = 0 < hi` stays PENDING (CP3-b, `lo >= 0`, is not adopted). **CP6** (deadline elapsed with an unavailable channel): the instance stays PENDING after `pending_until_ms` with `undecidable: <reason>:<signal>` (entries in the canonical order, joined by commas), never VIOLATED, no fifth state; the channel status enters through the seam `push_availability`. **CP7** (state across days): `run_offline` starts from an empty history (the first day of the patient, hypothesis H-hist); an instance whose `k_d` is after the closing step is decided early where rule 1 applies and is otherwise PENDING at trace end; there is no history input (ADR-046 is not implemented, so no negative times). **CP12** (infinite intervals): endpoints keep `-inf` and `+inf` in the raw interval and map to `-1` and `+1` in the normalised one; NaN is never emitted and an interval with NaN or `lo > hi` raises `MonitorInvariantError`.

The entry point `make_point_evaluator(template, instance, registry, step_ms, *, anchor_kind=None)` is recorded in the new ADR-053; ADR-013 itself is not edited.

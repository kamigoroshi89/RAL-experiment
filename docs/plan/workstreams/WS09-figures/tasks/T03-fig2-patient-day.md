## Obiettivo

Produce Fig. 2, one patient-day from the benchmark test split, generated by the system itself: normalised robustness per template over time, point measurements, the conformal band of the predicted robustness, the verdict states PENDING, PREDICTED-VIOLATION, VIOLATED (and SATISFIED at closure), the alerts with their attributed cause, and the injected events highlighted. It replaces the workshop Table I and must not reproduce any of its errors (analisi §4.6). Output: `paper/figures/fig2_patient_day.pdf` and `.data.json`, plus an exporter that writes the per-day trace the figure is drawn from into `outputs/fig2/<seed>/`.

## Test

`day_trace.json` content (defined by this task; all fields come from contract types): `patient_id`, `day_index`, `seed`, `care_plan_id`; `grid_ms` (monitor grid, step `T_s`); per template the unit, scale and priority read from the registry; `verdicts` (the `VerdictRecord` fields of `#verdict`); `predictions` (per instance and grid step while PENDING: predicted normalised robustness `sigma_hat`, conformal quantile `q`, `alpha`, `variant`, `emitted`); `measurements`; `observed_events` (DISPENSE, INTAKE, ROBOT_TASK, NODE_STATE, DEVICE_ERROR); `ground_truth` (the `GroundTruthEvent` fields); `alerts` (`t_ms`, `tau`, `kappa`, `sigma_norm`, `verdict`, `caused_by`, `root_cause_of`). The monitor input is the `ObservedDay` only; `ground_truth` is attached afterwards for drawing.

Fixture numbers are arbitrary and carry no clinical meaning: grid step 60 000 ms, one `resp` instance anchored at 3 600 000 ms with `pending_until_ms` 7 200 000 and `decided_at_ms` 5 400 000 (VIOLATED before the deadline because the interval upper bound became negative), one `sched` instance decided SATISFIED, one `count` instance that is a cascade effect of a C2 missed measurement.

Exporter (`tests/figures/test_fig2_patient_day.py`):

1. `test_export_selection_rule_is_deterministic`: on the fake manifest of three days, the rule (documented in the module: test-split day, in `(patient_id, day_index)` order, that contains injected events of all three classes and shows PENDING, PREDICTED-VIOLATION and VIOLATED for at least one instance; relax first the requirement of all three classes, then of PREDICTED-VIOLATION, and record which level was used) returns the same day on repeated calls; `--day patient:index` overrides it.
2. `test_export_passes_only_observed_day_to_monitor`: with a spy replacing `run_offline`, the object received is an `ObservedDay` and no `LatentDay` or `GroundTruthEvent` is reachable from its arguments.
3. `test_export_uses_smallest_seed_of_config`: with seeds `[3, 1, 2, 4, 5]` the exporter picks seed 1 and records it in `day_trace.json`.
4. `test_export_writes_provenance`: `outputs/fig2/<seed>/` contains `config.yaml` (copy of the source run), `git_rev.txt` (source run) and `day_trace.json`; the sidecar of the figure records the source run's revision and the current revision separately.

Model (pure function of the fixture):

5. `test_model_pending_instance_shows_interval_not_point`: while an instance is PENDING the model holds only its normalised interval (`sigma_norm_interval`), never a single value presented as the verdict (analisi §4.6 row 07:00).
6. `test_model_violated_marker_at_decided_at_ms`: the VIOLATED marker of the `resp` instance is at 5 400 000 ms, not at the anchor and not at `pending_until_ms`; for every VIOLATED or SATISFIED record the marker time equals `decided_at_ms` (analisi §4.6 row 21:02: a cross-modal verdict cannot appear before it is decidable).
7. `test_model_predicted_marker_only_while_pending`: PREDICTED-VIOLATION markers lie in `[anchor_ms, decided_at_ms or pending_until_ms]` and never after a definitive verdict.
8. `test_model_band_normalised_and_clipped`: for `sigma_hat = -0.2`, `q = 0.3` the band is `[-0.5, 0.1]`; for `sigma_hat = 0.9`, `q = 0.3` it is `[0.6, 1.0]` (upper edge 1.2 clipped to 1.0); all values in `[-1, 1]`.
9. `test_model_active_variant_edge_is_marked`: with `variant = "likely"` the upper edge (`sigma_hat + q`) is the emphasised edge; with `"possible"` the lower edge (`sigma_hat - q`) is.
10. `test_model_panels_ordered_by_priority`: panels follow the clinician priority order used by `rank_alerts`, and only templates with at least one instance in the day get a panel.
11. `test_model_panel_titles_carry_raw_unit`: each panel title names the template and the unit of its raw robustness (`RobustnessUnit`), while the y-axis is dimensionless (analisi §4.6: units were never declared).
12. `test_model_cascade_child_is_connector_not_top_level_alert`: the `count` alert with `caused_by` set is drawn as a connector to its root alert, and the top-level alert lane holds only the root (analisi §4.6 row 15:00; `#alert` semantics).
13. `test_model_injected_events_span_all_panels`: an interval `GroundTruthEvent` becomes a span over every panel with its class tag (C1, C2 or C3); a point event (`end_ms is None`) becomes a vertical line.
14. `test_model_dispense_and_intake_distinct_glyphs`: DISPENSE (open marker) and INTAKE (filled marker) are separate lanes or glyphs; no label contains "PILL".
15. `test_model_node_loss_shown_as_liveliness_event`: a robot or device failure is drawn from a NODE_STATE event of liveliness or heartbeat loss, not from a deliberate lifecycle transition (analisi §4.6 row 15:00; `#ros-2-interfaces`).
16. `test_model_time_axis_is_hours_since_trace_start`: x values are `t_ms / MS_PER_HOUR`; the label reads "h since trace start" (the clock time of trace start is not in the contract, so none is shown).
17. `test_states_distinguishable_without_colour`: the four verdict states use pairwise different glyph or hatch in the drawn objects.

Rendering and provenance:

18. `test_render_width_fonts_and_determinism`: width equals `FIG_WIDTH_DOUBLE_IN` (or single when `--width single`), fonts embedded, two renders give identical `.data.json`.
19. `test_sidecar_holds_caption_facts`: `.data.json` contains `patient_id`, `day_index`, `seed`, `alpha`, `variant`, `selection_rule_level`, `n_templates_shown`, so the caption written by WS12 quotes them without typing.

3. Implement `export_day.py`: read `outputs/main/<smallest seed>/config.yaml`, apply the selection rule to the test split, rebuild and fit the detector exactly as `run_experiment` does (same config, same seed; determinism is guaranteed by WS08-T07), run `run_offline` and the attribution step on the `ObservedDay`, attach the ground truth after the run, write `day_trace.json` through `write_generated`.
4. Implement `build_fig2_model` and make tests 5 to 17 pass. Choose the layout: small multiples stacked with a shared time axis (one axis per panel, no dual axis), panels for templates in priority order, then a lane for DISPENSE and INTAKE, then a lane for alerts with the attributed cause (C1, C2, C3, UNDETERMINED) in text. Encoding: robustness step line held between measurements with point markers at measurements; PENDING as a hatched span between anchor and deadline with the measured interval as a thin band; the conformal band as a separate lighter fill with the active variant edge emphasised; PREDICTED-VIOLATION as a triangle at the first emission, VIOLATED as a filled square at `decided_at_ms`, SATISFIED as an open check mark; injected events as neutral vertical spans with the class tag. Use the status palette of the dataviz skill for the states, validated with its script, and pair every state with its glyph or hatch.
5. Implement the renderer with `style.apply_rcparams()` and `style.save_pdf()` at double-column width by default (`--width single` supported); confirm the height fits the page budget of WS12-T02.
6. Run `python -m tools.figures fig2`, convert the PDF to PNG and inspect it: overlapping labels, unreadable hatches, band and line collisions, legend covering data. The legend is always present (two or more series) and direct labels are limited to four. Iterate.
7. Add the sidecar, run `ruff format`, `ruff check` and the tests until green. Commit `WS09-T03: add Fig. 2 patient-day` and set `status` to `done`. The caption (written by WS12) must state that the day is drawn from the semi-synthetic test split, how it was selected, and the alpha and variant, taken from the sidecar.

# B5 failure analysis: where conformal STL predictive monitoring fails on this problem

Task WS06-T06, analisi §5.4 (B5) and §10 objection 2. Companion of `B5-deviations.md` (the choices that define B5; deviation ids D1 to D21 are cited from there). B5 is the direct method of Lindemann et al. (ICCPS 2023, arXiv:2211.01539v2) [ref-needed: the bibliography entry is not verified, so no citation key is used] applied to the same templates, with the same forecaster class, the same calibration days and the same `alpha` as the proposed method. It is not weakened: every failure below comes from the setting, and the control shows the implementation working where the assumptions of the paper hold. All values are mechanisms on synthetic or hand-built inputs; none is a clinical claim, and coverage statements read "marginal over patient episodes under exchangeability". The micro-scenarios show that a mechanism exists; how often it bites on the benchmark is not measured here (section "Benchmark-scale evidence").

## Setup and reproduction

- Code: `src/ralmon/baselines/b5_conformal_stl.py` (`B5ConformalSTL`), `src/ralmon/baselines/raw_robustness.py` (adapter over `ralmon.monitor.make_point_evaluator`, `SignalGrid`). Both variants: `aggregation="conjunction"` (name `B5`, the primary one) and `aggregation="per_template"` (name `B5-per-template`, the strongest fair setting).
- Run: `.venv/bin/pytest tests/baselines/test_b5_conformal_stl.py tests/baselines/test_b5_failure_modes.py -q` (about 3 s). Fixed seeds: `numpy.random.default_rng(0)` in the control and in the sparse-gap test; no clock, no network; the Hypothesis test of causality uses its default database-free settings.
- Numbers quoted below come from those tests (the assertion values) or from re-running their helper functions with the same seed (the quantiles of the gap test). FIXTURE-ONLY values: band limit 140 mmHg, scale 20 mmHg, one dose, the registry F0 of `tests/fixtures/spec/registry_f0.yaml`; they are illustrative and unverified (analisi §4.1).
- What a run of B5 needs from the caller (found while writing the tests, relevant to WS08): `tail_ms` of the `TimingConfig` at least as long as the longest window of the templates (RESP: `delta2`; the day-end COUNT needs the run to reach 24:00), otherwise the instances do not close inside the run and the calibration episodes are dropped as censored (`n_censored`); a `plan_for_day` function for the calibration days (D14); empty carry-over between days (ADR-061, proposed).
- Agreement check with the monitor: `test_b5_measured_decisions_agree_with_the_monitor` runs a hand-built day through `run_offline` and through B5 without predictions: the first violated record of each of the seven instances and the seven per-template detections are at the same steps. B5 therefore repeats the instance creation and the signal construction of the monitor correctly on that day; the conjunction variant gives six detections (two instances violated at step 480 are merged).

## Sparse sampling

Mechanism. The direct method needs a trajectory predictor whose residual against the true robustness is small enough that `rho_hat + C < 0` can happen before the deadline (Theorem 1, equation (4), p. 8 of the preprint). The paper's evidence uses states at every time step (100 Hz in the aircraft case, Section 4.1, p. 11). Here blood pressure arrives a few times a day (analisi §4.3), so between the last measurement and the next the forecaster can only extrapolate hours ahead, the scores `R = rho_true - rho_hat` spread, and `C`, the `ceil((n + 1)(1 - alpha))`-th smallest score, grows. When `C` exceeds the excursion that matters, the predicted robustness never goes below `-C` for any reachable pressure, so the predictive part never fires and B5 falls back to the measured verdict at the deadline (B3-like).

Micro-scenarios and evidence.

- `test_failure_sparse_sampling_region_never_exits_safe_region`. Twenty residuals, five copies each of -50, -20, +20, +50 mmHg (test-local spread, assumed, not measured), `alpha = 0.1`, real `SplitConformal`: `C = 50` (`ceil(21 * 0.9) = 19`th of 20). For every predicted pressure from 140 to 190 mmHg (`sigma_hat` from 0 to -50) `sigma_hat + C >= 0` and nothing is emitted. With dense-sampling residuals (-5, -2, +2, +5), `C = 5` and a predicted 150 mmHg (`sigma_hat = -10`) is emitted. Through `B5ConformalSTL` on days built to give those residuals (RESP stub, persistence forecaster): with the wide residuals the first detection of a day that stays at 150 mmHg is the measured one at the deadline (score 10.0, `LENGTH` = 5 steps after the dose), with the narrow ones it is the alert at the activation step (score 5.0), five steps earlier.
- `test_failure_sparse_sampling_quantile_grows_with_the_gap`. A Gaussian random walk observed every `gap` steps, the dose between samples, `alpha = 0.1`, 300 calibration days, seed 0, persistence forecaster. Measured constant: 6.36 for `gap = 1`, 6.87 for `gap = 40`, 13.18 for `gap = 160` (the median absolute score goes 3.38, 3.60, 6.43). The growth is modest until the gap is long compared with the deadline window; it is not a cliff. The alert rates are not compared (the walk also drifts further from its start in the sparse case, which changes the population).
- `test_failure_small_calibration_set_never_emits`. Ten episodes at `alpha = 0.05`: `ceil(11 * 0.95) = 11 > 10`, the constant is `+inf`, and even `rho_hat = -110` is not alerted; the counters say so (`B5Prediction.quantile == inf`, deviation D19).
- `test_failure_sparse_sampling_no_predictive_part_for_event_templates`. SCHED, COUNT (day end) and INT depend on events and counters. The trajectory model of the paper (real-valued states, Section 2, p. 4) has no event predictor, so B5 asks the forecaster nothing for them (the forecaster stub counted zero calls, and a calibrator that would alert at any prediction produced no early detection): their detections are at the decision step only (D2). This is a structural gap: only RESP among the seven templates has a predictive part in B5 (`B5-deviations.md`, Mapping, table).

What the proposed method does instead. It forecasts the robot arrival for SCHED with the `DeadlineForecaster` (`02-interfaces.md#forecaster`, ADR-057), one prediction per pending instance at its activation (ADR-060), and keeps the predicted verdict formally apart from the measured one (PENDING and PREDICTED_VIOLATION never become VIOLATED by forecast, `#verdict`). Honest limit: for RESP the proposed method uses the same blood-pressure forecaster and meets the same wide residuals; the contract already says that WS08 reports this as a finding (`#forecaster`, Targets in scope). The difference that is specific to B5 is the missing event predictor and the missing separation of the verdicts.

What remains to be measured. The width of the real residuals of blood pressure on the benchmark, the fraction of patient-days on which the predictive part of B5 emitted anything, and the median constant relative to the band margin (`WS08-T02`).

## Mixed units

Mechanism. The conjunction of Appendix A (p. 24) takes the minimum of the robustness of the conjuncts. The templates have robustness in mmHg, ms and doses (`#template`, `RobustnessUnit`), so the minimum compares incomparable numbers, and a single calibrator pools scores of different units into one constant. The proposed method scales each template (`#template`, `normalise`) and calibrates in the normalised unit; `02-interfaces.md#template` forbids a raw minimum over units and the normalisation by the width of a conformal interval.

Micro-scenarios and evidence.

- `test_failure_mixed_units_raw_min_masks_dose_violation`. Twenty pooled episodes with `sigma_hat = 10` and `sigma_true` alternating 18 and 2 (scores +8 and -8, mmHg-sized), `alpha = 0.1`: `C = 8`. A dose over the limit has raw robustness -1 and `-1 + 8 > 0`: not alerted. Calibrated for the dose template in its own unit (scale 1, scores +0.5 and -0.5, `C = 0.5`), `normalise(-1, 1) = -1` and `-1 + 0.5 < 0`: alerted. This is a calibrator-level demonstration of the mechanism.
- `test_failure_mixed_units_raw_min_misranks_severity`. Raw values `range = -12` (mmHg) and `count = -1` (dose): the raw minimum is the range instance; with scales 20 mmHg and 1 dose the values are -0.6 and -1.0 and the minimum is the count instance. The same test shows the effect on detections: the conjunction variant gives one detection scored 12 for a step where a range violation and a dose violation coincide, the per-template variant gives two (scores 1 and 12, in the order of the instance keys, not of any severity).
- On the hand-built day of the agreement test, the conjunction variant merges the range violation (10 mmHg) and the cuff-integrity violation (SYS, a failed Boolean guard, robustness minus infinity) of step 480 into one detection whose score is the largest finite number (D20); the severity of the range excursion is not visible in it. The seven raw scores of that day (10, 1.8e308, 15, 5, 1, 33, 1.8e308 in mmHg, ms and doses) cannot be ordered by any clinical severity.

What was not reproduced, and why. At the level of the decision, the pipeline cannot reproduce the masking of a predicted alert by the pooled constant: in B5 only RESP has a predictive part (D2), so the pooled calibrator sees scores of one template and the pooled and per-template variants decide alike on predictions. The sign of a measured minimum equals the sign of any violation, so merging does not change which steps are alerted either. The mixed-units failure of B5 as configured here is therefore in the severity score, the ranking and the saturation at infinity, and in the loss of a separate detection for coincident violations; it becomes a decision failure for the prediction if any second template gets a predictive path (for example SCHED by a deadline forecaster, which the proposed method has). This is reported as found; B5 was not changed to produce the failure.

What the proposed method does instead. Per template scale and clipping to [-1, 1] (`normalise`, `#template`), lexicographic ranking by clinician priority (`rank_alerts`), calibration in normalised units per template (`#conformal-calibrator`, `CalibrationEpisode`).

What remains to be measured. How often coincident violations of different units occur on the benchmark, and the ranking agreement of the raw scores with the normalised ones (Kendall tau against the severity order, WS08-T02).

## No attribution

Mechanism. The paper has a verdict about the specification and no notion of a cause; the only attribution-like remark is the predicate-level analysis of the indirect method (Remark 4, p. 10), which B5 does not implement (D16). `Detection` has the fields `t_ms`, `label`, `score` and B5 sets `label` to `None`, so nothing in its output can carry a cause.

Evidence. `test_failure_no_attribution_same_range_detection_for_c1_and_c2_days`: two days with the same blood pressure of 152 mmHg at the same measurement; the second has `CUFF_HEALTH = 0.0` and a cuff `DEVICE_ERROR` event. B5 (RANGE template, real point evaluator) returns the identical detection in both: `t_ms = 5 * 60000`, `score = 12.0`, `label = None` (margin `min(152 - 110, 140 - 152) = -12`), and the evidence mapping passed to `run` is dropped. With the SYS template as well the faulty cuff changes the output (conjunction: one detection whose score saturates at the largest finite number because the failed guard of SYS has robustness minus infinity, D20; per-template: a second detection) and every label is still `None`: B5 sees that something is wrong and cannot say whether it is the patient, the device or the channel. `test_b5_ignores_evidence` checks that the output does not depend on the evidence at all.

What the proposed method does instead. The attributed cause κ in C1, C2, C3 or UNDETERMINED, with abstention (`02-interfaces.md#attribution`, `#alert`), computed from the evidence vector (`#evidencevector`). Faults that show in node health, device error codes or signal quality go to C2; silent faults producing plausible values remain indistinguishable from clinical deviations, which the diagnosability matrix reports (analisi §4.8, wording).

What remains to be measured. The recall of C2 and C3 events by B5 on the benchmark (it detects some of them as range or system violations) against the attributed ones of the proposed method, and the attribution confusion matrix, which is structurally empty for B5 (`attribution_confusion["B5"] is None` in WS08-T02).

## Other differences that are not failures

Documented, not tested as failures.

- Formal separation of the measured and the predicted verdict. B5 emits `Detection` only. After the deadline its decision is the sign of the measured raw robustness (equation (2) with nothing left to predict), and before it the prediction; there is no PENDING, PREDICTED_VIOLATION or three-valued interval, and no confidence `rho` on the alert (the proposed method returns `Rho` of kind MEASURED, DETERMINISTIC or PREDICTED, `#alert`).
- No allocation of `alpha` over the instances of a patient-day (D10). With `m` tested instances per day the expected number of false predictive alerts of a day can be as large as `m alpha`; the proposed method divides `alpha` by `m` (`BonferroniAllocation`). This raises the recall of B5 and its false alarms alike and is stated in the comparison.
- Detections are not nested in `alpha` (`test_b5_predicted_alerts_are_nested_in_alpha_but_detections_are_not`). Predicted alerts are (a smaller `alpha` gives a larger constant), but when the prediction stops firing the measured verdict at the deadline supplies the detection instead: on the worked day `alpha = 0.2` gives one detection at the activation step (score 4), `alpha = 0.1` the same step (score 2), `alpha = 0.05` (infinite constant) one detection at the deadline (score 10). A sweep over `alpha` therefore trades lead time, not the set of detected events; any check that expects the detections at a small level to be a subset of those at a large level fails for B5 by construction.
- No abstention and no ranking: every detection is final and unordered across units.
- Observed target: B5 calibrates on the observed completed day, with noise and injected faults inside the scores (D11); the same holds for every conformal method of this project, so it is not a difference.
- Day-local runs with empty carry-over (ADR-061, proposed): the 24 h dose counter under-counts across midnight for every baseline.
- The paper certifies satisfaction; B5 alerts on violation by the same theorem applied to the negated formula (D4). The literal rule "alert unless satisfaction is certified" was not run.

## Control: where B5 works

`test_b5_control_dense_exchangeable_days`. 200 calibration days and 2000 test days (`numpy.random.default_rng(0)`), each a dense Gaussian random walk (one value per step, common start 3 mmHg above the limit), persistence forecaster, one single-predicate template evaluated by the stub as `limit - value`, a dose at step 20 and a deadline at step 40, `alpha = 0.1`. The error event is the one of `#conformal-calibrator` rule 3: an alert at the activation step and a true robustness not below zero. Measured: alerts on 22.75 % of the test days, error rate 0.85 %, constant 6.25; the assertion bound is `alpha + 3 sqrt(alpha (1 - alpha) / 2000) = 0.1201` and the test also requires an alert rate of at least 5 % so that it is not vacuous. The implementation therefore works where the assumptions hold (dense states, exchangeable trajectories, a single unit), and the failures above come from the setting. The bound is loose on this population because most test days are far from the boundary; the rate is below `alpha` for the reason given in the test docstring, not because the guarantee is sharp. `test_b5_decision_rule_worked_example` fixes the boundary by hand (`rho_hat + C = 0` is not an alert).

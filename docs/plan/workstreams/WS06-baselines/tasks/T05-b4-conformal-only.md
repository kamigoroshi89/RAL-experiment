## Obiettivo

Implement `B4ConformalOnly`: conformal anomaly detection that raises a detection when a measurement leaves the conformal prediction interval of a forecaster, with no STL template, no band and no schedule knowledge (analisi §5.4 B4). It isolates the contribution of the specification: whatever B4 misses that the proposed method finds is due to having a clinical specification. Concrete output: `src/ralmon/baselines/b4_conformal_only.py` and its tests, built on the public API of `ralmon.conformal` (the same calibrator code as the proposed method).

With an `allocation` the level of every signal is the allocation of `alpha` over `n = len(signals)` tests, taken from `per_test_exact` (a `Fraction`) when the allocation has it, else from `per_test` (the protocol has only that; the stub of test 6 has only that). **Deviations from the task text and choices where the sources are silent (most conservative reading).**
1. Residual index: `(t - t_prev) // step_ms - 1` (hand-over from WS05-T05), not the `floor((t - t_from_ms) / step_ms)` written in WS06-T03. A gap shorter than one step has an empty path: no residual, counted in `run_stats.no_forecast` (a floor index of 0 would not exist). WS06-T03 (B2) must use the same index and the same skip, or WS06-T07 test 11 (residual series of B2 and B4 equal) fails.
2. The forecaster is called as `predict(history_of_the_signal, t_prev, gap, signal=...)` (the keyword only if the signature accepts it), with the history of that signal delivered so far, before the current sample. A path that is not the one asked for (signal, issue time, length, finite value) is a `ValueError`, not a silent skip (B5 skips it); `InsufficientHistoryError` skips the residual and counts it (an autoregression of order 2 loses the second sample of a day: `test_b4_real_forecasters_agree_with_the_stub_and_the_autoregression_runs`). `ForecasterNotFittedError` for a signal with no model (an autoregression fitted on days without that signal) propagates: it is a configuration error, not a quiet day.
3. The grid, the lateness rule and the pre-day rule are those of B1 (copied inline, a shared helper would need a change outside "File toccati"): a sample received after its step is final is dropped and counted (`late_inputs`), exact boundary and reorder delay pinned; a device time in `(t0 - step, t0]` is step 0 of the day, an earlier one is a delivered value (it can be the previous sample) that gives neither a detection nor a calibration residual. A duplicate is delivered twice (no deduplication) but has gap 0, so no residual and no second alarm (counted in `not_newer`; B1 would raise a second alarm). A non-finite value is not a reading (`non_finite_samples`).
4. The run ends at the last step that delivers a monitored sample (`max(t_rx, t_device + D)`), not at `evaluation_end_ms`: B4 does not read `plan.schedule`, so the output cannot depend on the plan or on `tail_ms` (tests 12 and `test_b4_does_not_depend_on_the_tail_of_the_timing`). `read_log` stays empty.
5. A signal with no calibration episode (fewer than two samples on every calibration day) gets a calibrator from the factory but no `fit`; B4 is silent on it and `run_stats.uncalibrated_signals` names it. A quantile that is NaN or negative is refused (`ValueError`); a quantile of 0 gives the score `sys.float_info.max` (the rule of B5); `+inf` never alarms.
6. Detection time is the delivery step (`t_eval + D`), not clipped at midnight (`test_b4_detection_after_midnight_is_not_clipped`); the open decision of WS06-T04 (clip or keep) applies to B4 as well. B4 runs one day at a time with empty state (ADR-061), so the first sample of every day has no residual.
7. `PLACEHOLDER_TEMPLATE = TemplateId.RANGE`. It has no meaning; any `TemplateId` would do. A consumer that filters calibration episodes by template would wrongly read them as RANGE episodes (finding for WS06-T07 `## Findings`, together with the absence of a template field in `Detection`).

Beyond the task: `B4Residual` rows of the last run (`residuals`: signal, delivery step, device time, value, forecast, residual, the quantile used and the alarm), `calibration_episodes`, `n_calibration_episodes`, `run_stats` (`B4RunStats`), `signals`, `unit`, `alpha`, `level`, and the staleness check (`StaleCalibratorError` if `calibrated_for != forecaster.model_version`, test `test_b4_stale_calibrator_is_refused`).

**What B4 sees and what it cannot see (also in the module docstring; the class lines are expectations derived from the design, not measurements).** Sees: samples of the monitored signals, a forecaster, the quantile of calibration residual magnitudes. Reads nothing of the plan or the registry (`read_log` empty, `access_log` shows `observed` reads only, `denied` empty, `test_b4_calibration_reads_observed_only`). It sees a CHANGE between two consecutive readings of one signal, in either direction and in the unit of the signal; it does not see a level, an absence, an event, a count, a schedule, a robot task, or the clinical meaning of a change. Pinned: `test_b4_blind_spot_a_constant_level_is_never_an_anomaly` (a value held at 200, a climb in steps below `q`, an extreme first reading), `test_b4_blind_spot_events_obligations_and_absences` (dispenses, measurements as events, an hours-long gap with unchanged values), `test_b4_alarms_on_any_large_change_in_either_direction_whatever_its_clinical_meaning` (a drop alarms like a rise: false alarms by construction). Per template of the proposed method: RANGE only as a large change into or out of the band, never as a level; HOLD, COUNT, SCHED, RESP, SYS and INT not at all. Per class: C1 an abrupt spike yes (when larger than the usual change), a refused or missed measurement, a missing response to a dose and a double dose no; C2 noise on a continuous channel yes, a detached or silent device, a stuck value (residual 0), a delayed or failed robot task and a dispenser fault no; C3 injected or replayed values only if they differ from the previous reading by more than `q`, forged dispenses and spoofed presence no. WS08 must report B4 per class and per subtype and must not present its recall on C2/C3 as a statement about conformal prediction: for most subtypes B4 has no channel on which the violation appears. Blood pressure is a poor target (about five readings a day): its gaps are hours long and the persistence residual over such a gap is as large as the usual variation.

**Statement of the guarantee (qualified).** For `unit="day_max"`: the probability that the largest residual magnitude of a test day on a signal exceeds `q` is at most `alpha`, marginal over patient episodes under exchangeability of calibration and test days, a fixed forecaster, and exchangeable numbers of readings per day (the daily maximum grows with the number of readings); not conditional on the patient. The allocation is a union bound over the monitored signals of one patient-day. `unit="sample"` does not follow rule 1 of the contract and the statement does not apply to it. The calibration days are unlabelled, so injected anomalies inflate the quantile (shared by any conformal method without labels). Days of one patient are not independent and the harness does not check the split by patient.

**Cost.** The forecasters filter and sort the whole history of the signal at each call, so a day costs O(n^2) in the number of readings: a 24 h day with one reading a minute on 3 signals (4320 samples) runs in about 0.85 s, five such days fit in about 4 s. Acceptable at benchmark scale; if WS08 needs it faster the fix is a bounded history window (not added: the window that a forecaster needs is not in the `Forecaster` contract).

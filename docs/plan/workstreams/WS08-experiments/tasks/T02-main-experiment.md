### Decision of 2026-10-11 (gab; ADR-074): attribution is a probability per cause

### Hand-over from WS08-T09 (worker, 2026-10-12): metric definitions of ADR-074 and what is not wired yet

Layout and definitions are in the README of WS08 ("metrics.json layout", keys `attribution_log_score` to `attribution_labelling`) and in the Notes of WS08-T09. In short, for the main experiment:
- **Log score and Brier** of `Alert.p_kappa` against the class of the cause of the alert, PER TEMPLATE (RESP apart, the aggregate is the plain mean of RANGE, SCHED, HOLD, COUNT, SYS, INT that have an alert, ADR-072); log score in nats (`-ln p` of the true class; a zero probability gives a null mean with a reason and `mean_finite`), Brier `sum over C1, C2, C3 of (p_c - y_c)^2`. Calibration curves: pooled one-vs-rest bins with counts per template (an empty bin is `n: 0` and null, never an accuracy of 0), per class, and top label with its ECE.
- **Sets** (amendment 1): coverage of the true subtype and mean size per template, true class and true subtype, coverage per patient with the cluster bootstrap over patients; the hierarchical rule's kappa is scored as a set (UNDETERMINED is the full set of the applicable subtypes). **Equal coverage** (amendments 2 and 3): each method's posterior is calibrated by `SubtypeSetCalibrator` (marginal and Mondrian per template) on the calibration part of the patient-level split to the same `set_alphas` and compared on the test part by mean set size, with `top1_fill` and the Mondrian fallbacks per template and the role-swap check next to it; the pairs get a SEPARATE calibration with the transfer from the main population next to it.
- **Pairs**: `Lambda = log P(e|s_A) - log P(e|s_B)` between the two specific subtypes, prior-free; indistinguishable stratum `D = 0` exactly and `|Lambda|`; distinguishable stratum the direction, McNemar between methods on the pair; no precision.
- **Labelling**: TIGHT is primary and LOOSE next to it in every table (the choice was made after seeing the result). For the three point C2 subtypes (`nav_delay`, `manipulation_failure`, `dispenser_bounce`) the tight label of an alert the loose rule attributes to them is AMBIGUOUS (own bucket, never the natural C1 cause) and the headline figure is the LOOSE one (`attribution_labelling.injected_subtype_table`, zeros shown). `run_options.attribution.loose_after_ms` is an `awaiting_human_value` with no default and also controls the C1 and C3 events under the tight labelling.

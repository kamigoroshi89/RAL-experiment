## Obiettivo

Produce the validation report of a benchmark version and the alert-load sanity check, so that the benchmark can be inspected and not merely trusted (analisi §10 objection 1: "rilasciare il generatore, così la circolarità è ispezionabile"). The report checks structure, leakage, fidelity to the priors, response consistency with the registry windows, class balance and realisation of injections, confounder pairs, provenance, determinism and the alert load of the monitor on a null run and on the full observed days. It invents no acceptance threshold: it reports numbers and flags structural failures. Concrete output: `validation.py`, the `validate` subcommand, `docs/benchmark/validation-report.md` (generated) and tests.

## Test

Checks are numbered V1 to V12 in the report. Statuses: `PASS`, `WARN`, `FAIL`, `NOT_RUN`. Small fixture builds only; fixture-only values. Write first.

1. `test_report_lists_every_check_with_status`: the report has an H3 heading and one status per check V1 to V12.
2. `test_report_is_deterministic`: two runs give identical bytes; no timestamp, host or user name.
3. `test_report_header_marks_provisional`: a manifest with `n_unfilled > 0` or `n_unverified > 0` gives a header line `PROVISIONAL` with both counts; `fixture_only` gives `FIXTURE-ONLY BUILD`; a fully filled and verified prior gives neither.
4. `test_leakage_check_fails_when_truth_key_in_observed_file`: an observed file with an added `ground_truth` key makes V2 `FAIL`; the clean fixture passes.
5. `test_split_check_fails_on_patient_in_two_splits`: a manipulated manifest makes V1 `FAIL`.
6. `test_latent_identical_with_injections_off`: regenerating two patient-days with all injections disabled gives latent bytes equal to the built ones (paired design, T03 stream independence); a test double whose latent depends on the injection makes V2 `FAIL`.
7. `test_prior_fidelity_recovers_sampled_parameters`: re-sampling the patient from the prior and generation seed, the latent baseline, AR SD, lag-1 coefficient and circadian amplitude are recovered within tolerances computed from the sample counts (four standard errors); an altered latent file makes V3 `FAIL`.
8. `test_responder_rate_reported_and_warns_when_zero`: the fraction of virtual patients whose PK/PD onset window overlaps the registry window is reported; a prior whose peak effect stays below δ gives 0 and V4 `WARN` (structural inconsistency between generator and registry).
9. `test_realisation_rates_reported_per_class`: with 4 injection records of which 3 changed the trace, the realised fraction is 0.75; unrealised injections are listed, not counted as events.
10. `test_low_count_subtypes_flagged`: any `class/subtype` with fewer ground-truth events than `min_events_for_reporting` is listed in a `WARN` of V5.
11. `test_pairs_reported_with_indistinguishable_count`: V6 lists pair counts per group and variant and how many are `indistinguishable_by_construction`.
12. `test_alert_load_null_run_equals_c1_truth`: on a two-patient hand fixture, the null run (same latent, perfect observation, no injection) gives VIOLATED records per patient-day equal to the C1 truth per patient-day, exactly; a stub runner that adds one spurious record makes V9 `FAIL`.
13. `test_alert_load_table_schema`: per template the table gives records per patient-day, records that overlap a truth event of the same template and records that do not (observation-induced); matched plus unmatched equals the total.
14. `test_multi_seed_mode_reports_min_and_max`: with 5 generation seed directories every numeric check reports its minimum and maximum; with fewer than 5 the report carries a `WARN` "fewer than 5 generation seeds".
15. `test_rebuild_check_detects_nondeterminism`: rebuilding one patient from the manifest settings equals the stored files; a test double that consumes the clock makes V10 `FAIL`.
16. `test_noncircularity_audit_lists_safeguards_and_existing_tests`: V11 lists the safeguards of the workstream README and every test name it cites exists in `tests/benchmark/test_ground_truth.py`.
17. `test_cinc2015_recorded_not_run_without_data`: with no dataset directory, V12 is `NOT_RUN` with the reason; no number is invented.
18. `test_validate_cli_exit_codes`: `validate DIR` exits 0 when every check is `PASS`, `WARN` or `NOT_RUN`, exits 1 when any is `FAIL`, and writes the report to `--report`.

### Decision of 2026-10-11 (gab; ADR-071)

The validation report must declare the realistic BACKGROUND of natural events and not look for a clean day: natural events per patient-day by template (`spontaneous_range`, `spontaneous_resp`, `spontaneous_hold`, `spontaneous_count`), the noise alarms (observed out of band while the latent value is in band), the share of days with no truth event, the alarm rates of B1 and B3 without injections, and the realised fraction and effective k per injected subtype. False alarms are reported on WINDOWS WITHOUT INJECTIONS (outside every injection window and its causal consequences), never on 'clean days'. The injected C1 subtypes are reported as the EXCESS over the background. `ecg_noise` is excluded from this version (its section says so).

### Hand-over from WS04-T16 (worker, 2026-10-11): what the validation report must report for the anchored cuff faults (ADR-070)

The windows of `cuff_detached`, `cuff_silent_bias` and `stuck_at` are anchored to k = 1, 2, 3 consecutive readings (`docs/benchmark/builder.md`, "Cuff faults anchored to the readings of the day"). The report must give, for each of the three subtypes: (1) the REALISED FRACTION, `realised / placed`, from `manifest.settings.anchored_cuff_faults.realised` (virtual patients, scored days) or recomputed from the `injection_log` entries of the truth sidecars, together with the draws that the plan could not place (`settings.plan_skipped`: `c2/<subtype>/overlap`, `.../too_few_readings`, and `c2/stuck_at/first_day` for the day-0 exclusion); (2) the EFFECTIVE k: the distribution of `details.n_readings_covered` against `details.n_readings_drawn` per subtype (`anchored_cuff_faults.readings_drawn_to_covered`, keys such as `"2->1"`), and every per-k figure (detection, sensitivity, the bias grid) by the covered k, never by the drawn level; (3) the exclusion of day 0 for `stuck_at` and the share of `stuck_at` windows that changed nothing (readings equal to the frozen value); (4) the zero-offset row of the bias table (ADR-070 (f)): the alarm rate of B1 without any fault, against which the 3 x 3 cells are read, and the declared properties of ADR-070 (c) (no cell is detected with certainty by a band rule; cells that never reach z >= 2 are sub-threshold by construction; a constant offset on both the pre-dose and the post-dose reading of one dose cancels in the RESP drop). The expected value of the realised fraction on a fault-free day is 1 for the three subtypes (days 1 to 6 for `stuck_at`); a lower value in the built benchmark is the overlap of windows of the same subtype, an earlier device fault that removed the readings, or the robot stage, and must be explained, not hidden. The observability table of `builder.md` is generated by `python tools/anchored_observability_table.py`; the report can cite it.


### Addendum from WS04-T16 (worker, fix round 2026-10-11): the reading ledger and what counts as a positive

(1) The ledger of the three anchored cuff subtypes is in `details` of every `injection_log` entry: `n_readings_drawn`, `n_readings_lost_robot` (of which `n_readings_removed_robot` dropped by `manipulation_failure`, the rest moved out by `nav_delay`), `n_readings_gained_robot`, `n_readings_lost_device` (removed by an earlier `cuff_detached`) and `n_readings_covered`; the manifest tallies it per subtype and drawn k (`anchored_cuff_faults.by_drawn_k`). Report the two causes apart: the robot share is a property of the robot fault rate and delay levels, the device share a property of the C2 rate (anchoring puts every cuff window on the same five readings, so windows of different cuff subtypes overlap). A link fault (`ble_dropout`) runs after the cuff faults and is not in `covered`. (2) A placed fault with `n_readings_covered` 0 is unrealised: no ground-truth event, not a positive in any count; `settings.plan_unrealised` (`c2/<subtype>/covered_zero`) counts them next to `plan_skipped` (the draws that were not placed, among them `c2/stuck_at/first_day`). The report's realised fraction per subtype is `realised / placed` of `anchored_cuff_faults.realised`; the planned count is `placed + plan_skipped`. (3) The ground-truth interval does not shrink with the effective k: the truth event of a realised fault is the spec window `[onset, end)`, and only `realised` and the ledger carry the effective k, so a per-k figure must take k from the log. (4) The day-0 C2 injection rate is 5/6 of the other days (a `stuck_at` drawn there is drawn and not placed) and the planned `stuck_at` count is 6/7 of the others'; the other subtypes keep their rate. The measurements behind (1) are in `docs/benchmark/evidence/scripts/anchored_loss_build.py` and `anchored_loss_report.py` (FIXTURE builds).

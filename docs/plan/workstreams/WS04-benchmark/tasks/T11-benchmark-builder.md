### Hand-over (worker, 2026-10-09, from the WS05-T04 review, round 2)

Correction of the gate in the hand-over above: it said to require `presence_level_older_than_window_steps == 0`, which cannot be met (the generator's dwell of 10 minutes exceeds `int_window` of 5, so any visit counts, with or without marks). The gate is `stats().presence_samples_without_availability_marks == 0`: non-zero exactly when presence samples were used and no availability mark for `presence` ever arrived. Emit the robot `NODE_STATE` reports (ADR-054 option (b)) or the explicit zeros (option (a)) and read that counter from `run_offline_detailed`.

Time axis and plan anchors of `run_offline`: the days of a patient are written on one continuing axis (`day_index * T_day + clock time`, WS04-T06), and `run_offline` now runs the day `day_index` from the step `day_index * K` on that axis (it used to run steps 0 to K whatever the day, so a day with `day_index >= 1` was accepted with 0 inputs and no error). Consequences for the builder: (1) the times of day `d` must be on the continuing axis (a day written on the per-day axis with `day_index >= 1` has every input rejected as finalised, visible in `stats().after_finalisation_count`); (2) the plan may list the instances of other days: those anchored before the first step of the run are skipped and counted in `stats().plan_instances_before_start`, those anchored in the finalised past of the run still raise `PlanFeasibilityError`; the day-end COUNT of day `d` has the anchor `(d + 1) * T_day`; (3) there is no carry-over between days (ADR-046 is not implemented): the 24 h counter and the held levels start empty at the first step of the run, so a counter that depends on the previous day under-counts. The contract text of `TimeMs` ("since the start of the patient-day trace") reads as the per-day axis; ADR-053 (h) asks the plan owner to settle the axis in `02-interfaces.md`.

### Hand-over from WS05-T11 (worker, 2026-10-10)

The monitor reads the reports now (`StreamingMonitor.push_event`, so `run_offline` too). What the builder must write, checked against the code: (1) an `Event(EventKind.NODE_STATE, t, t_rx, "robot", {"state": "active", "colocated": True|False}, seq)` per arrival (true) and per departure (false); the source must be exactly `"robot"` (`vocab.SOURCE_ROBOT`), otherwise the report is ignored and counted in `stats().colocated_reports_ignored`; the value must be a real `bool`. (2) `ralmon.benchmark.vocab` does not declare `colocated` yet: `PAYLOAD_KEYS[EventKind.NODE_STATE]` is `{state, commanded}`, so `check_payload` would refuse the key; add `KEY_COLOCATED` there (and to `__all__`). (3) The mask starts as "available until the first report" (not H-INIT unavailable), so write an arrival report for every visit, including the first of the day, at or before its presence sample. A presence sample used before the first report of the run does not witness later dispenses (the held level is cleared at that report) and is counted by the gate, so a dropped first arrival report (a C2 fault of the report stream, an injector that removes events) makes `presence_samples_without_availability_marks` positive on that day: keep it in mind in the builder and in the injectors, and report such days instead of filtering them silently. (4) The gate `presence_samples_without_availability_marks == 0` is satisfied by the first applied report of either value; a day with no visit has no presence sample and passes trivially. (5) A report with the same device time as the presence sample of the arrival is fine (marks are applied before samples at one device time).

**Hand-over from WS05-T05 (ADR-057).** `nav_eta_ms` and `task_queue_ms` (`SignalId.NAV_ETA`, `SignalId.TASK_QUEUE`, integer milliseconds, refreshed at least once per monitor step) must be emitted for the deadline forecaster (`DeadlineForecaster`) to be fitted and used: without them every `sched_tick` of a training day is counted as having no robot state and `fit` raises `ValueError`.

### Hand-over from WS04-T15 (worker, 2026-10-10): the real signature of the labeller with history

`assemble_benchmark_day(observed, latent, plan, records, behaviour_log, run_fn=None, config=None, latency_bound_fn=None, mode="scheduled", *, registry=None, templates=None, history=())` and the same keyword on `label_latent_day` and `label_latent_day_detailed`. `history` is a sequence of `ralmon.benchmark.labelling.HistoryDay(latent, records=(), behaviour_log=())`, one per earlier day of the same patient, in increasing `day_index` order, at most `MAX_HISTORY_DAYS` (14; one day is enough with the registry of today). Per patient-day the builder passes the latent day of day d-1 with the injection records and the behaviour log OF THAT DAY (the same lists it gave to the call that labelled d-1), and `plan` as one care plan for the whole record of the patient (a SCHED instance for every tick of every day and the day-end COUNT of every day, anchors on the continuing axis): a per-day plan labels the day but the SCHED deadlines that cross midnight do not exist for the monitor. What changes for the builder: (1) the sidecar of day d lists the records of day d only; an event of day d that a record of day d-1 explains (the morning COUNT after a forced evening `double_take`, the 01:00 RESP after an evening `drug_nonresponse`) is `injected = True` and appears in no `truth_indices` of day d (`LabelledDay.history_record_index` holds the link, `AssembledDay` does not carry it): V1, V2 and V5 of WS04-T12 must not assume that an injected C1 event has a record in its own day. (2) Ownership (ADR-064): a record decided at the shared step of midnight belongs to the day that starts there, the day-end COUNT stays with the day it closes. CORRECTION (review of WS04-T15): the last day of each patient has no next day, so EVERY record the monitor decides at the final closing step is in no truth, and the monitor and the baselines run on that day do emit them (WS08 would score false positives; 76 of 200 seeded patients had at least one). It is not covered by "keep the last doses at least `delta2` before midnight": that advice is wrong at the boundary (a dose at exactly 21:00 has its RESP deadline at 24:00), and it ignores SCHED ticks within the window of midnight and a HOLD or COUNT-limit violation of a dose in the last minute. Nothing is decided after the closing step, so the earlier wording "at or after 24:00 or later" was inexact. See `docs/benchmark/ground-truth.md`, "The last day of a patient". TWO POLICIES, BOTH PROPOSALS FOR GAB AND THE CHOICE IS OPEN: **P1** (recommended by the reviewer and the coordinator): the builder generates one extra unscored TAIL day per patient, labelled with the history of the scored days, so that the closing step of the last scored day is owned; the monitor and baseline runs stay unchanged; **P2**: WS08 drops the detections decided at the final closing step of each patient, identically for all methods, and loses the real events there. This task must NOT silently pick one: record the question for gab (or wait for his decision) before writing the builder. (3) The assumption "a day is labelled with empty history" of the T10 hand-over is false from now on; the per-day truth is a strict subset of the history truth on the seeded patients of the tests (4.5% to 9.6% of the events; HOLD at a dose within the look-back of midnight is the exception, case HC-06). (4) `observed` must still be the `ObservedDay` of the day itself; the history is latent only (the labeller builds the ideal observation of each history day and never reads an observed day).

### Decision on the last day of a patient (gab, 2026-10-10; ADR-066)

The tail-day policy P1 is decided. The builder generates `n_days + 1` days per patient: the last one is the unscored tail day, generated with the same machinery, labelled with the history of the scored days, and marked in `manifest.json` (`tail: true` or an equivalent field described in `docs/benchmark/`). Only the records the tail day owns at its first step (the shared step) are the truth of the closing step of the last scored day; all its other events are not scored; the monitor and the baselines are not run on it. The scale of the build (200 patients x 7 days) is 7 scored days and one tail day per patient. Add to the Test section, written first: a test that the manifest marks exactly one tail day per patient, that the tail day is not in any split count or day total, and that the truth of the closing step of the last scored day equals the first-step truth of the tail day (the example of `docs/benchmark/ground-truth.md`, "The last day of a patient": a dose at 21:00 with the window closing at 24:00). Also keep in this task the other hand-overs (parameter records for B1: RANGE, HOLD and COUNT with `anchor_ms` None, so that `rules_active == ("R1","R2","R3","R4")`; the plan must cover the whole record; the sidecar link to a record of the previous day).

### Fixes after the independent review (worker, 2026-10-10; status still `in_progress`)

Review verdict FAIL with one blocker and nine minors; the red tests are `e022992` and the fixes follow it.

* **Blocker, twin ids reveal the mechanism.** Fixed. A twin has the id shape of a virtual patient (`p0042`); the id numbers of all patients are one permutation drawn from the generation seed (`BuildContext.planned_ids`), so ids, care-plan ids (`plan-<id>`), directory and file names carry no role, and member A is the lower id in about half of the pairs. The A/B label, `pair`, `group`, `variant_id` and `mechanism` are in the manifest and the truth side only. Tests: `test_twin_ids_carry_no_role_and_have_the_shape_of_a_virtual_id` (scans the names and contents of `days/` and `plans/` for `pair`, `-A`, `-B`, the roles and every mechanism name) and `test_twin_ids_are_interleaved_and_the_assignment_is_drawn_from_the_seed`. The split stays by unit (a pair is one unit). Side effect: the ids of a build depend on the seed, so two seeds no longer give the same set of virtual ids per role (the set of ids is the same).
* **Minor 2, `verify`.** It now refuses a file on disk that the manifest does not list, parses every observed file with `read_observed_day` and `check_payload`, and refuses an observed file that names latent data, ground truth or the injection vocabulary (record fields, subtype names, group names, template ids, `spontaneous_`, `injection`). * **Minor 3.** `test_the_indistinguishable_flag_is_the_computed_one` pins the flag to `evaluate_pair` on a pair where it is False (`nominal_content_robot`) and one where it is True (`stress_nocontent_norobot`); the mutant `flag = True` fails it.

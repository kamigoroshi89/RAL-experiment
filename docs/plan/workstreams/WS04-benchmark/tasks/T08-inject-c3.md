## Obiettivo

Inject C3 integrity attacks (ADR-002) against the declared attack classes of the threat model, at two attacker-knowledge levels: replay of the previous day's measurements, injection of plausible pressure values, forged dispense event, simulated presence (analisi §5.1 (3)). Attacker capabilities are the flags defined by WS02-T02, not chosen here. The informed attacker who is coherent on every available channel is generated and labelled as undetectable by construction: it stays in the ground truth so that the diagnosability limit is reported as a result, not a margin note (analisi §10 fourth objection). Concrete output: `inject_c3.py`, the C3 part of `injections.yaml`, tests.

Modelling decisions (module docstring of `inject_c3.py`, tested).

- **Level.** The level of a spec is `spec.level` (`naive` or `informed`, required for `replay`, `dispense_forgery` and `presence_spoof`, implied by the name for the two injections, recorded in `InjectionRecord.level` and the log). There is no per-flag mix through the pipeline (`attack_signature` accepts any combination for the tests of the table). The duration level of the design grid (`short`, `medium`, `long`) is not recorded in the spec.
- **Relay position (H-SUPP).** A cuff attack suppresses every message the cuff delivered in the half-open window on `t_rx_ms` (its four samples and its `MEASUREMENT`, `MEASUREMENT_MISSED` and `DEVICE_ERROR` events; node reports, robot events and other devices stay: H-EVID). Nothing is added to the evidence sources: the attacks never write a `quality`, a `DEVICE_ERROR` of a device, a `NODE_STATE` or a `ROBOT_TASK` (TM-OP-14, evidence steering, is not modelled).
- **Replay.** The `MEASUREMENT` messages of the previous day (the observed stream in `InjectionContext.previous_day`; same patient and seed, `day_index - 1`, else `InjectionContextError`; day 0 is an `InjectionSpecError`) whose arrival plus one day lies in the window are delivered at that instant, whatever today's cuff did (it works inside a dropout of the genuine device, test 15). Naive: verbatim (stale stamp, previous number, the pulse as sent). Informed: stamp equal to the arrival, continuing numbers, and with `coherent_pulse` the pulse is the ECG rate held at the instant (last sample at or before it; if no ECG sample exists the pulse stays). Without a message to replay nothing is suppressed and the injection is not realised. Failure reports of the previous day are not replayed.
- **Injection.** One forged message in place of each genuine `MEASUREMENT` message of the window, at its instant, stamp equal to the arrival. Naive: uniform draws in the bounds of the context (`bp_bounds_mmhg` from the registry names `bound_bp_lower` and `bound_bp_upper`, `hr_bounds_bpm` from two rows of the YAML that wait for a human; the injector refuses to run without them), pulse an independent draw, no group samples, numbers from `FORGED_SEQ_START` (a forger that ignores the counter starts its own from 1; per spec). Informed: uniform between the minimum and the maximum of the genuine `bp` samples captured before the onset plus the whole previous day (none: not realised, never an invented range); the pulse is the ECG rate; health, inflation and quality as the genuine message had them; continuing numbers. Draws come from `(seed, patient, day, INJECTION_C3, injection_sub_index)`, a fixed layout of two columns per message.
- **Informed numbering.** The forged messages take consecutive numbers from the smallest number of the suppressed messages (else the number after the last earlier message of the device's sequence, else `cuff_start`), every later message of the device shifts by `forged - suppressed`, and `settings["next_cuff_seq"]` follows (the relay renumbers for good, so the next day continues). Attacker traffic that ignores the counter (a stale replay, a naive forger) is recorded in `InjectionWork.unsequenced` and is never renumbered. Inside the window the numbers are consecutive: a gap that a link dropout left there is closed.
- **Dispense forgery.** One synthesised `DISPENSE` of the dispenser, no payload, at the onset (a point). Naive: its own number from `FORGED_SEQ_START` (the first one no dispense at that instant uses). A device without numbers gets none. The witness fields of the signature come from the stream (fix round 1): they read False when a robot visit covers the instant and no robot `INTAKE` lies in the visit; the informed forgery alone in front of a visiting robot is therefore not tagged `expected_undetectable`, and is once the companion `presence_spoof` with `spoof_intake` (which ranks first) wrote the intake, or when the robot is away (superseded: the first version assumed the companion).
- **Presence spoof.** A `presence` sample of 1.0 at the onset only if a robot visit covers the instant (`work.visits`, closed interval; otherwise the channel is `None` and the injection is not realised); with `spoof_intake` an `INTAKE` of source `robot` at the same instant. No identity event: the benchmark robot has no identity check.
- **Genuine view.** The pipeline freezes `InjectionWork.genuine_view()` when the attack stage starts; an attack reads history, the ECG rate and (for numbering) nothing else from it, so two attacks never read each other's traffic and the result does not depend on the order of the specs. Windows of `replay`, `injection_naive` and `injection_informed` on the cuff must not overlap (`Injector.overlap_group`); touching windows are allowed.
- **`n_changed`** counts items changed (a replaced reading is one), removed, added, renumbered and logged; an injection that changes nothing leaves the stream exactly as it was.

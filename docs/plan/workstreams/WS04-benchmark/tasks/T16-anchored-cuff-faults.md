## Obiettivo

Implement the decisions (a), (b), (d) of ADR-070 in the benchmark builder and the C2 injectors: the windows of `cuff_detached`, `cuff_silent_bias` and `stuck_at` cover k consecutive readings of the day (k = 1, 2, 3), the truth records the effective k, and `stuck_at` can freeze the last reading of the previous day (the first scored day of a patient is excluded from its draw). `estimate_pd` is a rough planning figure, not a measurement.

## Test

Write first.

1. `test_a_window_covers_exactly_k_readings`: for each of the 5 / 4 / 3 feasible first readings of k = 1 / 2 / 3 on the default schedule, every (onset, end) pair of the rule covers exactly k readings and ends before the day end (exact enumeration, as in the research report).
2. `test_realised_share_is_one_for_detached_and_bias`: the real injectors realise every anchored `cuff_detached` and `cuff_silent_bias` window on a fault-free day.
3. `test_stuck_at_uses_the_previous_evening`: with `previous_day` the frozen value is the last reading of the previous day for a window before the first reading of the day; without it (day 0) the draw excludes `stuck_at`; the realised share is 1 on days 1 to 6 (anchored) and the manifest records the exclusion of day 0.
4. `test_effective_k_after_the_robot_stage`: when a robot-stage fault removes a reading inside the window, the record carries `n_readings_drawn` = k and `n_readings_covered` = k - 1, and the truth uses the effective one.
5. `test_duration_rows_are_in_readings`: the rows load with unit `readings` and the values 1, 2, 3, ordered, within 1 and the number of readings of the day; a row in ms for these subtypes is refused with a named error; the other subtypes still require ms.
6. `test_the_plan_is_deterministic`: two builds with the same seeds are byte-identical; a change of the drawn k changes only the affected injection.
7. `test_observability_table_matches_the_generator`: the exact distribution of readings per window for each k equals the committed table of `docs/benchmark/builder.md` (the table is regenerated by a script from the generator, not typed).

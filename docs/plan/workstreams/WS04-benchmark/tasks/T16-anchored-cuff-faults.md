Judgment calls (conservative reading in each):

1. **Day 0 and `stuck_at`: draw and skip, not redraw (confirmed by gab, fix round).** The reading was: the subtype is still drawn uniformly among the six, and a `stuck_at` that falls on day 0 is not placed and is counted as `plan_skipped["c2/stuck_at/first_day"]` (the pattern of the replay on day 0). The alternative, drawing day 0 among five subtypes, would raise the rate of the other five subtypes on day 0 by 6/5. The cost is that `stuck_at` has 6/7 of the planned count of the other subtypes (the sample-size arithmetic of `group1-design-options.md` section 3 assumed it was drawn on day 0 and lost 1/(6-k) of its windows).
2. **(Superseded by the fix round below: the injectors now write the whole ledger.) `n_readings_drawn` is counted, not carried.** `InjectionSpec.params` takes only string or bool choices and the spec is a frozen contract, so the drawn k is not stored in the spec. The builder adds `n_readings_drawn` = the readings of the fault-free base stream (after the C1 refusals, before every injection) inside the window, which equals the k of the rule by construction (tested). For a spec that the plan did not draw (a scripted one) it is the count of base readings in the window.
3. **`n_readings_covered` is counted by the injector when it runs.** That is after the robot stage (ADR-070 (b)) and also after an earlier device fault: a `cuff_detached` window that overlaps a `cuff_silent_bias` or `stuck_at` window removes the readings that the later fault would change, so the later record can show `3->1` or `1->0` (observed in a 40-patient fixture build, e.g. `cuff_silent_bias` `1->0` 3 times of 14). The overlap rule of the plan only forbids windows of the SAME subtype. Every per-k report uses the covered number.
4. **A reading is a `bp` sample instant** of the day, deduplicated; the readings of the planner are those inside `[day start, day end)`. Two readings in the same 60 s grid cell cannot be isolated by a window, so a first-reading choice that has no grid instant for its onset or its end is left out (it never occurs on the default schedule). The "6 - k" of the ADR is `n - k + 1` for a day with n readings; a day with fewer than k readings gets no window (`plan_skipped["c2/<subtype>/too_few_readings"]`).
5. **Loader.** The rows of the three subtypes must be in `readings`, integers of at least 1, strictly increasing from `short` to `long` (the ADR says "ordered"; strict is the conservative reading and the committed 1, 2, 3 satisfies it); the block unit must be `readings` too; every other duration must be `ms`. The builder refuses (`BenchmarkConfigError`) a row above the number of measurement ticks of the schedule. `consumer_task` of the nine rows stays `WS04-T07` (the existing structure test pins it).
6. **`stuck_at` that changes nothing is unrealised.** If every covered reading equals the frozen value the injection leaves the stream as it was (the convention of the injectors); with a continuous cuff error this does not occur, and T18 (rounding) must decide whether it matters (written in its Notes).
7. **The previous evening is read from `InjectionContext.previous_day`**, which the builder fills with the post-injection stream of the day before: a reading lost to a link fault or a detached window is not the one frozen (an earlier reading of that stream is). A same-day earlier reading always wins over the previous evening. A previous day with no reading leaves nothing to freeze (unrealised).
8. **The tail day** is planned like any other day (it has a previous day), so it draws `stuck_at`; it is not scored.
9. **The ks of the observability table** are read from the duration rows, not fixed in the code.

Closed form per reading: `manipulation_failure` 0.71% / 2.38% / 4.05% at rates 0.3 / 1.0 / 1.7 (rate/6 per day times 1/7 for the tick); `nav_delay` moved out 0.06-0.9% (only the last covered reading can leave, when the delay passes the window end); device loss of bias and stuck 2.1% / 7.0% / 11.6%. The robot removal and the device loss agree with the closed forms within the intervals (the bias at k = 2 and 3 runs above it at rates 1.0 and 1.7: 11.3% against 7.2% at k = 3, rate 1.0). Answer: the loss is not a property of the 40-patient trial (its 3 of 14 is inside the interval of the 16% expected at its rate 1.0), it is a property of the injection RATE of that trial (1.0, against 0.3 by default: the loss grows linearly with the rate; at 0.3 the share of draws with covered != drawn is 2-7% and the covered-0 share at most 1.2%) and of an INTERACTION that the earlier estimate left out: the estimate counted the robot stage only, per tick, and assumed that a cuff window meets nothing else. Anchoring puts every cuff window on the same five readings, so a `cuff_silent_bias` or `stuck_at` window meets a `cuff_detached` window of the same day with probability rate/6 per reading times about 0.26-0.57 (the coverage of a detached window), which at rate 1.0 is 2-3 times the robot loss; the uniform windows of the old design almost never met each other. The robot-anchoring interaction (a delayed reading leaving a window that is tight around it) is small (0.1-0.9% per reading). A draw of k readings also multiplies the per-tick figure by k (mean 2). Not tallied: a `ble_dropout` that later deletes a message of a covered reading (it does not change `covered` or `realised`; the reading is simply absent from the observed day).

Review minor items done: `tools/anchored_observability_table.py` puts `src` on the path and the imports in the `try`, so it works with plain `python3` or exits 2 with a message; `_check_reading_rows` raises `BenchmarkConfigError`; `test_effective_k_after_the_robot_stage` covers the three subtypes (removal and entry of a reading); the interval of the truth event does not shrink with the effective k (builder.md, T12 Notes). Edge cases: (a) if a `cuff_detached` window has removed the earlier same-day readings, a `stuck_at` window freezes the PREVIOUS EVENING's value (`frozen_from` = `previous_day`) although earlier readings existed in the base day; (b) `previous_day` is the post-injection stream of the day before, C3 attacks included: if the last `bp` sample of the evening was forged or replayed, `stuck_at` freezes that delivered value, not the one the device measured.

## Obiettivo

Complete the latent day and add the C1 injections of analisi §5.1 (3): hypertensive spikes, drug non-response (reduced Emax), hypotension below the hold parameter, and patient measurement refusal. Then build the **deliberate confounders** of §5.1 (4) as matched pairs that share the same base day and seed and differ only in the labelled mechanism: real non-response versus forged dispense, double take versus sensor bounce, and (from R3-W4, a third group added by this plan) patient measurement refusal versus robot no-show. C1 injections change the latent day; they produce injection records, never ground-truth events, because C1 truth is computed by the labeller on the latent trace (T10). The pairs include variants that are observationally identical once the discriminating channel is withheld, so that abstention can be scored. Concrete output: `inject_c1.py`, `confounders.py`, `latent_day.py`, the C1 and confounder parts of `injections.yaml`, tests.

## Test

Fixture-only values: constant baseline SBP 130 with zero AR noise; one dose at 08:00; PK/PD fixture of T04 (`ka = 2 per h`, `ke = 1 per h`, `Emax = 20 mmHg`, `EC50 = 0.25`); intake lag 5 000 ms. Write first.

1. `test_hypertensive_spike_adds_magnitude_inside_window_only`: a spike of 40 mmHg over `[t1, t2)` gives 170 inside and 130 outside, exactly.
2. `test_hypotension_pulse_subtracts_magnitude`: magnitude 40 gives 90 inside, 130 outside.
3. `test_drug_nonresponse_scales_emax_for_intakes_in_window`: the effect at `t_max` is 13.3333333 unscaled, 6.6666667 with scale 0.5 and 0 with scale 0; an intake outside the window keeps its full effect.
4. `test_measurement_refusal_removes_latent_and_observed_measurement`: the latent MEASUREMENT event at the tick is absent; the observed stream has the robot visit and a declined-task event and no `bp` sample.
5. `test_c1_injectors_return_records_not_ground_truth`: the C1 entry point returns `InjectionRecord` values with `cls = C1` and no `GroundTruthEvent` is constructed in `inject_c1.py` (checked by an AST scan).
6. `test_latent_day_has_no_observation_only_fields`: the assembled `LatentDay` contains no `cuff_health`, `inflation_ms`, `presence`, no `quality`, and only the event kinds DISPENSE, INTAKE, MEASUREMENT (ideal) and SCHEDULE_TICK.
7. `test_latent_measurements_at_scheduled_ticks_with_exact_values`: latent MEASUREMENT events sit exactly at the schedule ticks and the latent `bp` value at a tick equals the held latent trace value.
8. `test_assembly_without_injections_equals_baseline_minus_effect`: bit-for-bit equality with `compose_latent_sbp(baseline, pkpd_effect)` (paired baseline, T03 and T04).
9. `test_forced_double_take_matches_markov_double_take`: forcing the double-take state for a slot produces the same latent events as the chain in that state with the same gap.
10. `test_magnitude_levels_are_relative_to_named_reference`: a spec level 2 with reference quantity 20 mmHg resolves to 40 mmHg; the YAML contains no absolute mmHg magnitude for C1 (fixture check of the file structure).
11. `test_double_take_vs_bounce_pair_shares_base_and_seed`: both variants have equal latent baseline, cuff noise draws and schedule; only the mechanism differs.
12. `test_double_take_vs_bounce_identical_when_discriminators_withheld`: with content sensing off, the robot absent and the bounce gap set to the double-take gap (stress level), the two observed dispense streams are equal while the latent days differ (two intakes against one) and the records are C1 `double_take` against C2 `dispenser_bounce`, both with group `double_take_vs_bounce`.
13. `test_double_take_vs_bounce_separable_with_sensing_or_interval`: with content sensing on the second DISPENSE carries `content_change` 1 in the double take and 0 in the bounce; at the sub-second nominal gap the interval alone separates them.
14. `test_nonresponse_vs_forgery_identical_without_witness`: real non-response (C1 `drug_nonresponse`, scale 0) and informed forged dispense with spoofed witness (C3 `dispense_forgery` plus `presence_spoof`) give equal observed streams when the robot is co-located and spoofed, and equal streams without witness when the robot is absent; the latent days differ (a dose against none).
15. `test_nonresponse_vs_forgery_separable_by_witness`: with the robot present and a naive attacker the forged variant lacks the INTAKE event that the real variant has.
16. `test_refusal_vs_robot_missed_pair`: C1 `measurement_refusal` against C2 `manipulation_failure` at the same tick: both lack the measurement; the robot task outcome differs (declined against failed); group `refusal_vs_robot_missed`.
17. `test_confounder_group_carried_by_records_of_both_members`: both records of a pair carry the group string; ordinary injections carry `None`.
18. `test_pair_records_flag_indistinguishable_by_construction`: the injection log of each variant has `variant_id` and `indistinguishable_by_construction`, computed by comparing the observed streams of the pair up to the declared discriminating fields.
19. `test_c1_and_confounder_rows_have_provenance`: every C1 parameter row and every variant-grid row of `injections.yaml` has `source_type` and `source_ref`, and at least three magnitude levels exist per C1 magnitude.

**Modelling choices (module docstrings, all tested).**

- **C1 changes the latent day and returns records, never truth.** An AST scan of `inject_c1.py` and `latent_day.py` forbids the names `GroundTruthEvent`, `record_to_ground_truth`, `ground_truth_events`. A C1 record has `evidence_paths` empty and `confounder_group` from its spec; `realised` is `n_changed > 0` (an injection with no grid point, no intake in the window, a scale of 1, or a tick that is not a tick of the day is logged unrealised). A forced `double_take` is always realised when the tick is a dose tick (it explains the latent double take even if the chain drew the same state); its record instant is the surplus dispense (`tick + double_take_gap_ms`), the refusal's is the tick, the windows' are `[onset, onset + duration)`. The labeller (T10) decides what is true.
- **Windows** are half open (fault-model convention), on the integer-ms latent grid, inside the day of their onset (a window that crosses midnight, a negative duration or onset, a scale outside [0, 1], a non-positive pressure are `C1InjectionError`); two windows of one subtype may not overlap, touching is allowed; different subtypes overlap and add (spikes first, then hypotensions: exact, order-independent). Pulses are added to `baseline - effect`; with no pulse the pressure is bit-identical to `compose_latent_sbp(baseline, effect)`.
- **Magnitudes are `level x reference`**, resolved by the caller: `scale_range` (registry) for the spike, `hold_distance_mmhg(baseline, bp_hold_threshold)` for the hypotension, the dimensionless Emax scale in [0, 1] (absolute, reference 1) for the non-response. No clinical constant in the code (the package-wide guards pass); `c1_spec_from_level` stores `reference`, `multiple`, `reference_value` in the spec params and refuses a magnitude that disagrees.
- **Continuity across midnight.** AR noise and adherence chain use streams keyed per day (`(seed, patient, day, LATENT_AR)` and `(seed, patient, day, ADHERENCE)`), not the patient-level `adherence_stream` of T05 (it would restart at the first draw in a call that starts at day `d`); the carried state is the last noise value, the last natural chain state and all earlier true intake times (pkpd has no memory: the scale of an intake is a function of its time and the spec list, so pass the same list to every call). A 3-day call equals three 1-day calls that carry `end_state` (tested with noise, a custom chain with memory, a dose 10 min before midnight whose second intake falls after midnight, and a drug that spills into the next day). An intake after midnight stays in the events of the day of its slot and acts from its own time.
- **Forced slots without touching `adherence.py`.** The chain runs as usual (every draw consumed), then the log entry of the forced slot is replaced by the entry the model gives for the point-mass chain in the forced state on a one-dose schedule (the public API; the draws of a dummy `CONFOUNDER` stream are ignored) and the day's events are rebuilt from the log (`_events_from_log`, tested equal to the model for every state). The natural chain continues underneath (forcing is an intervention). `ForcedSlot` allows ON_TIME and OMITTED (scenario controls, no record); a double take is the C1 spec `double_take` (an injection, with a record); DELAYED needs a draw and is refused.
- **Latent day**: samples `bp` (latent grid), `hr_ecg` and `spo2` (from `draw_clean_vitals` with the observed base's key, equal to the observed ones, tested); events `SCHEDULE_TICK` (source `ward`, payload `kind`), ideal `MEASUREMENT` (source `cuff`) at every tick except the refused ones, true `DISPENSE` (`dispenser`) and `INTAKE` (`patient`). No `cuff_health`, `inflation_ms`, `presence`, quality or seq. `LatentResult.baseline_sbp_mmhg` (pressure before drug and pulses) is a generator-side sidecar for the paired design, not part of `LatentDay`.
- **Refusal on the observed side**: `build_observed_base(..., skip_measurement_ticks_ms=result.refused_ticks_ms)` (the robot still visits, no reading, no other draw moves) then `observe_refusals` adds one `ROBOT_TASK` (task `manipulate`, outcome `declined`, stamped at the arrival), against `manipulation_failure` (`failed`, stamped at the reading instant).
- **Pairs** (`PairBase` = the base day and seed shared by both members; one day, day 0): the members share patient, baseline, cuff noise draws, natural adherence draws and schedule; only the labelled mechanism differs. Group 1: A = C1 `double_take`, B = C2 `dispenser_bounce` after a forced ON_TIME dose (gap = nominal design row or the double-take gap). Group 2: A = forced ON_TIME + C1 `drug_nonresponse` scale 0 over the dose's slot, B = forced OMITTED + C3 `dispense_forgery` (+ `presence_spoof` with `spoof_intake` at `tick + intake_lag` for the informed level with a robot). Group 3: A = C1 `measurement_refusal`, B = C2 `manipulation_failure` at the same tick. **`refusal_vs_robot_missed` is the third group, added by this plan from R3-W4** (not one of the two groups named in analisi 5.1 item 4).
- **`indistinguishable_by_construction` is computed** (`compare_observed`): the OBSERVED days of the members, per declared discriminating field (`content_change`, `witness` = INTAKE events and the presence level over each robot visit, `robot_task_outcome`, `dispense_interval`, `dispense_seq`, `verification`) minus the fields the variant withholds, plus `other` (everything else the day holds); empty means indistinguishable. Presence is read as a level held over the visit (an extra 1.0 inside a visit is no difference, one outside every visit is); the order of items at one instant is no difference; the function reads `ObservedDay` and the robot visits only (a test checks it). Pressure samples are ignored for groups 1 and 2, where the physiological response differs by construction (two doses against one; a dose whose drug still occupies the saturation term of a later dose against no dose, ADR-039), and `PairEvaluation.pressure_response_differs` reports it; group 3 keeps them. Each record of both members gets `variant_id` and the flag in `details` and in `confounder_log` entries.
